# Support and Moderation Playbook

Reports enter a generic moderation queue with target type, restricted target reference, reason, reporter, created time, status and audit history. Triage threats, exposed private information and impersonation first; acknowledge within one business day, urgent privacy issue within four hours. Moderator may hide, warn, mute, suspend or ban with reason and expiry; author receives safe notice and appeal route. Keep reviewer identity separate from review content and restrict access.

Takedown requests identify URL/content and claimant basis; preserve evidence with limited access, hide content pending urgent risk review, decide and log reason, notify parties, and purge/anonymize under retention policy. Do not disclose anonymous reviewer identity. Escalate credible legal threats to founders/counsel. No moderation action may reveal marks or private schedules.
