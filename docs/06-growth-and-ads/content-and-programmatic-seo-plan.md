# Content and Programmatic SEO Plan

## Page families
University hub → departments → stable course catalog pages; opt-in TA profile; public assignment guide; editorial blog/help; calculator landing pages; general TA tools. Generate a page only when source fields are complete, content is distinct and useful, and publication review passes. Keep unpublished/thin pages out of sitemap.

## Editorial workflow
Brief and keyword intent → subject-matter draft → factual/policy review → moderation/legal check where needed → metadata/schema preview → publish/schedule → revalidation and sitemap → 30/90-day Search Console review. Course data is factual catalog data, not student submissions. UGC is moderated and nofollow.

## Calendar
Monthly publish one evergreen study/calculator explainer, one verified help article, and refresh pages with policy/date changes. Course and TA pages are data-driven only from approved public fields. Avoid fabricated rankings, scraped course notes, duplicate city/term combinations, or claims of affiliation.

## Measurement
Track indexed eligible pages, organic clicks/impressions, query coverage, engaged sessions, pages/session, return visits, calculator completion/share, signup conversion, CWV, consent rate and ad RPM by page family. Use Search Console and GA4 after consent/appropriate configuration. Pause indexing when content becomes stale or source is withdrawn.
